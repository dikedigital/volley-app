Funnel Meta Ads Testing Plan
Funnel One-Pager

Nine steps from cold traffic to paid subscriber.

The funnel is built around one structural insight: the AI leveling call is the product's strongest conversion moment. Every step before it earns the call. Every step after it converts the experience into a subscription.

Acquisition Funnel

Step by step

1

Paid Ad

Target: cold traffic, 28–45, prior language learning experience. Three audience angles, tested in sequence. The ad sells one thing: a free conversation that diagnoses exactly where your language breaks down. It does not mention $99. It does not explain the product. It earns the click.

2

Landing Page

Single-scroll page. One CTA. Opens with the failure the buyer already knows. Promises a diagnosis in 15 minutes. No pricing. No feature list. The page's only job is to move the visitor into Step 3.

3

Pre-qualification (3 questions)

Language. Current level. Primary goal. These three inputs route the AI call correctly. Email is captured here — after question 3, before the call confirmation. Capturing email at this point creates a retargeting anchor for users who drop before completing the funnel.

Assumption: Three inputs are sufficient for the AI to personalise the call opening.
4

AI Leveling Call (15 minutes)

A real adaptive conversation in the target language. The AI assesses level, identifies specific production gaps, and generates the data for the results screen. Runs in-browser. No app download required at this stage. Fully AI-run. No human involvement.

Assumption: The AI produces genuinely adaptive conversation, not a scripted flow. This step is the highest-risk assumption in the funnel. If the AI call feels scripted, the results screen loses credibility and conversion drops.
5

Results Screen

Three sections, in sequence:

Assessment. Level confirmed with 2–3 specific observations from the call. Specificity is the conversion driver here — generic level labels do not create urgency.

6-week plan. Three focus areas tied to the goal stated in Step 3. Dynamically generated, not templated.

Offer. $99/month introduced here for the first time, anchored against the personalised plan. 30 days free, no credit card required. First human coach session in week one.

Assumption: The AI generates specific, non-generic observations. Assumption: 30-day free period requires no credit card upfront.
6

Account Creation

Name, email (pre-filled), password. One screen. Immediately followed by: "Your first coach session is being scheduled. You'll hear from us within 24 hours." This confirmation message is not optional — it sets the expectation that a human is now engaged and creates a forward commitment before the user has closed the tab.

Assumption: Coach scheduling can accommodate new users within the first week of signup.
7

Email Sequence (3 emails)

Covers the gap between account creation and the first human coach session.

Email 1 (immediate): Restates the assessment. Links to the first AI practice session.
Email 2 (24 hours): Coach has reviewed the assessment. Confirms first session focus.
Email 3 (48 hours before first session): One practice prompt to do before the call.

Assumption: Email 2 requires a real coach reviewing the AI call summary, or copy that does not imply it. If no human review happens at this stage, Email 2 is cut.
8

First Human Coach Session

The coach arrives with the AI call summary and any practice data from the preceding days. The session opens on the specific gap identified in the assessment — not an introduction. This session is the primary retention driver for the 30-day trial period. Its quality determines conversion to paid more than any other variable in the funnel.

Assumption: Coaches have technical access to AI call summaries before sessions. This is a hard product requirement, not a nice-to-have.
9

Trial-to-Paid Conversion (Day 28–30)

Two emails at day 21 and day 28. Frame: "here is what you accomplished in 30 days" followed by "here is what the next 30 days look like." The ask is not to avoid losing access. It is to continue something that is working.

Assumption: The platform generates enough usage data in 30 days to make this email specific. Users with zero sessions require a separate re-engagement flow before day 28.
Risks

Key assumptions at a glance

Step Assumption Risk if wrong
4 AI call is adaptive, not scripted Results feel generic, conversion drops at Step 5
5 AI generates specific observations Diagnosis moment fails, offer lands cold
5 30-day free trial requires no credit card Friction and trust loss at the highest-intent moment
6 Coach scheduling within first week Email sequence loses coherence, drop-off increases
7 Coach reviews AI summary before Email 2 Credibility risk if implied but not true
8 Coach has access to AI call data First session feels generic, trial-to-paid rate drops
Summary

The critical path

Steps 4, 5 and 8 carry the funnel. The AI call creates the insight. The results screen converts it into intent. The first human session converts intent into payment. Every other step is infrastructure supporting these three moments. If any of the three underperforms, optimising the ad or the landing page will not fix the problem.
