Funnel Meta Ads Testing Plan
Meta Ad Angles

Four angles. One offer.

All four sell the free AI call — not the subscription. No angle mentions price. No angle mentions a free trial. Every ad ends at the same place: a 15-minute conversation, no account required.

4 ad angles · 3 cold traffic angles · 1 retargeting angle ·
1
Cold traffic Video or static App dropout

The person who has quit before

28–45. Has tried Duolingo at least twice. Considers themselves "bad at languages" but has decent passive knowledge. The failure was never ability — it was accountability. No one noticed when they stopped. The app sent a notification, and that was it.


This person does not need to be sold on language learning, they already want it. They need to be convinced that this time is structurally different, not just better.

Hook logic
What happened when you quit Duolingo? Nothing. What happens when you skip with Volley? Someone notices.

That asymmetry is the entire ad. The proof point is the coach message: specific, human, adaptive. Not an automated nudge — a real response to what actually happened. No app has that. The hook names the exact failure mode this person has already lived and shows the structural difference in one screenshot.
Ad copy
I quit Duolingo twice.

Both times, nothing happened. No one noticed. The streak reset and the app sent me a notification the next day like nothing was wrong.

With Volley, my tutor messaged me when I skipped Tuesday.

Not an automated email. A real message: "I see you missed yesterday — want me to set up something shorter for today so you don't lose momentum?"

I did the shorter session. I haven't missed a week since.

What to test
A

"I quit Duolingo twice." — Personal identification, first person. Self-selects harder.

B

"The problem with every language app is the same: no one notices when you stop." — Direct category accusation. Casts wider.

Optimize for: Call start rate, not just CTR.
2
Cold traffic Static or video Prior spender

The person who has already spent money and stopped

30–50. Has spent real money on language learning — a course, a tutor, a premium app. Stopped not because they ran out of motivation but because the ROI felt wrong. They kept showing up but could not measure progress. The tutor was fine but generic. The course had a curriculum that did not match what they actually needed. They are not burned out on language learning. They are burned out on paying for things that do not move the needle.


This person is not resistant to spending money. They are resistant to spending money before knowing what the actual problem is.

Hook logic
Every other product asks them to buy first and figure out later whether it was the right fit. Volley inverts that. The free AI call is a diagnosis before a prescription. The hook names the pattern they already know — spending without knowing what to spend on — and offers a way out of it that costs nothing.

The promise is not "Volley is cheaper" or "Volley is better." The promise is: find out what the actual problem is before spending another cent. That argument works on people who have not hired a tutor yet too, not just those who have.
Ad copy
You have probably spent money on language learning already.

Apps. Maybe a course. Maybe a tutor for a few months. Something that felt like progress and then stopped.

Before you spend anything else, find out what the problem actually is.

Most people who struggle with a language are not bad at languages. They have a specific gap — a verb tense they avoid, a situation where they freeze — and they have never had anyone name it precisely.

Volley's AI tutor runs a free 15-minute conversation in your language.At the end: your level, the specific gaps between what you know and what you can produce, and a plan that targets them.

No account. No credit card. Just find out where you actually are.

3
Cold traffic · Best fit Video or static Intermediate plateau

The person who knows the language but freezes

25–45. Intermediate. Can read and understand more than they can produce. When someone speaks at normal speed, they understand 60–70% — but respond in half-sentences, apologize, and switch back to English.


The failure is not vocabulary. Not grammar. It is the gap between passive understanding and active production under time pressure. They know the word. They cannot get it out when someone is looking at them.

This person does not identify with "beginner." Do not position Volley as beginner-friendly to this audience. They will ignore it.

Hook logic
Name the exact failure moment: someone speaks, they understand, they still freeze.

That is not a knowledge problem — it is a reps problem. They have never practiced producing language under social pressure. Apps do not create social pressure.

The promise is not "get fluent." The promise is: you already know more than you think. Find out precisely where production breaks down.

This angle fits the funnel best of the four. The AI call is the exact thing this person needs: a real conversation that surfaces exactly where they stall. The ad, the page, and the call are one continuous argument.
Ad copy
You know the word.

You know you know it. You've seen it a hundred times. But someone asks you a question in Spanish and it's just... gone. You smile and say "sorry, my Spanish isn't very good" and the conversation moves on.

That's not a vocabulary problem. You have the vocabulary.

What you don't have is reps under pressure. Real conversation pressure, where someone is waiting and you have to produce.

Volley runs a free 15-minute AI conversation in your language. It responds, waits, and adapts — and at the end tells you exactly which constructions you avoid, which situations make you stall, and what to do about them. 

Free. No account. See what your actual gaps really are.

Optimize for: Call completion rate and trial-to-paid (not volume).
4
Cold + Retargeting Video only All audiences

Product demo — the thing itself

All audiences. Runs as a cold traffic entry point and as a retargeting layer for visitors who saw Angles 1–3, clicked through, but did not start the call. 


Works at multiple funnel stages because it does not rely on a specific pain point — shows the product and lets the viewer self-qualify.

Hook logic
Every competitor's creative is a person smiling while holding a phone. Nobody shows what actually happens inside the product.

The hook is the absence of a hook — just the product itself. No emotional setup, no problem-agitation, no category comparison. Just: this is what a Volley session looks like. The viewer either recognizes the value or does not. The ones who do are the right buyers.

For an early-stage product, honesty is a competitive advantage.
Ad copy
This is what a Volley AI conversation looks like.

15 minutes. At the end: your level, your specific gaps, a plan.

Free. No account required.

Deployment order

1

Run Angles 1, 2, 3 simultaneously on cold traffic. Equal budget. Single format per angle. All traffic goes to the new landing page. Do not change the page during Phase 1.

2

After Phase 1 kill decisions, reallocate to the winning angle. Kill date is day 21 — not day 10, not daily. See the Testing Plan for full kill logic.

3

Launch Angle 4 as retargeting against all page visitors who did not start the call.

4

Test Angle 4 on cold traffic only after the retargeting signal is established.
